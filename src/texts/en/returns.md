We sell cosmetics — goods that are subject to special hygiene requirements under the law. Below is what can and cannot be returned, and what to do if something is wrong with your order.

## Products of proper quality

### Opened packaging

Cosmetics with broken packaging seals cannot be returned or exchanged. This is expressly provided for by the Law of Georgia "On Consumer Rights Protection" and is due to hygiene requirements: we cannot guarantee the safety of a product that has already been opened.

### Unopened products delivered to you

If you ordered on the website, Instagram, WhatsApp or Telegram and received the order by delivery, you may return an unopened product within 14 calendar days of receipt, without giving a reason.

Conditions:

- the packaging is unopened, protective film and stickers are intact;
- the product has not been used and looks as new;
- you have the receipt or order number.

In this case, the buyer pays for the return shipping. We refund the money within 14 calendar days of receiving the product back, using the same payment method. Usually it takes us about a week.

### In-store purchases

Products of proper quality bought in the shop cannot be exchanged or returned: you had the opportunity to inspect and choose them on the spot. If in doubt, ask for a tester — we have them for almost everything.

## Defective products

If a product is defective, damaged in transit, does not match the description, is the wrong item or has expired, we will replace it or refund your money. Opened packaging does not matter in this case. We cover all costs, including shipping both ways.

Message us within 14 days of receipt and attach:

- photos of the product and packaging;
- a close-up photo of the problem;
- the order number or receipt.

We reply within 2 working days and offer a choice: a replacement with the same product, an exchange for another product with the price difference adjusted, or a refund.
