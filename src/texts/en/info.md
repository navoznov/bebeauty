<h2 id="order">How to order</h2>

You can place an order on this website, on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/bebeauty_ge/), [WhatsApp](https://wa.me/995593759113) or [Telegram](https://t.me/BeBeautyGe). After you place it, our manager will contact you to confirm the details.

Not sure what to choose? We offer a free consultation and will help you find the right routine for you.

<h2 id="payment">Payment</h2>

Payment is made by bank transfer to a TBC account (legal entity) after the order is placed — the manager will contact you with the details. Cash payment is available on pick-up.

If you don't have an account with a Georgian bank, you can pay through a TBC payment terminal. Unpaid orders are held for 48 hours.
